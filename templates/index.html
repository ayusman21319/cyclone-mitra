BEFORE THE CYCLONE

Prepared today. Safer tomorrow.

A few practical steps can reduce risk. Follow local administration instructions and evacuate when authorities advise.

01
⌂

Secure your home

Bring loose outdoor items inside. Keep doors and windows secured. Stay away from damaged electrical lines.

BEFORE
02
▣

Build an emergency kit

Keep drinking water, dry food, medicines, a torch, batteries, first aid and copies of important documents.
